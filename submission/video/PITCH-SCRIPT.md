# x9 protocol — Pitch Video Script
## 8 scenes · ~2:27 · Dami's voice · Gemini Charon for reference read

---

## [SCENE 1 — HOOK — 15s]

Crypto markets run every hour of every day. The average trader does not. That gap between what the market offers and what any person can actually respond to is the product opportunity.

---

## [SCENE 2 — PROBLEM — 25s]

Most trading tools give you a template. You pick from a menu, you set a number in a database somewhere, and when something goes wrong you have no way to verify whether the bot followed your intent or just did whatever it wanted. The enforcement is advisory. Nothing about it is verifiable on-chain.

---

## [SCENE 3 — SOLUTION — 25s]

x9 lets you deploy an autonomous AI agent on Solana in under two minutes. You describe your strategy in plain English, Claude Sonnet converts it into a cryptographic policy enforced on-chain by Swig before any transaction touches your funds. Claude Haiku then monitors thirty-one tokens every five minutes and trades within those exact constraints. You never touch it again.

---

## [SCENE 4 — MOAT — 20s]

What makes this defensible is the enforcement layer, not the AI layer. Swig's policy runs at the transaction level before the chain processes anything. Every agent also gets a permanent on-chain identity through Metaplex Core, Bonfida SNS, and Ika MPC key management. A competitor who wants to copy this needs the same on-chain primitives, not just the same interface.

---

## [SCENE 5 — MARKET — 15s]

Solana moves between one and four billion dollars in DEX volume every day. Millions of active traders are already on the network, already have wallets, already understand the tooling. The adjacent market is small crypto funds without engineering teams who want automated execution without sending funds to a CEX.

---

## [SCENE 6 — GTM — 15s]

The product generates shareable output by default. P&L curves, block events, Claude's exact reasoning on every trade. That is exactly the content Solana Twitter engages with, and Phantom and Metaplex give us ecosystem distribution from day one.

---

## [SCENE 7 — REVENUE — 12s]

Per-agent subscription, fifteen to thirty dollars a month. A thousand active agents is between one-eighty and three-sixty K in annual recurring revenue, and trade volume take-rate scales that further.

---

## [SCENE 8 — CLOSE — 20s]

I'm Dami, building this full time. x9 protocol is a new product category — autonomous agents with cryptographic policy enforcement, not a feature bolt-on to an existing trading platform. The moment to define it is now. x9-protocol.vercel.app.
